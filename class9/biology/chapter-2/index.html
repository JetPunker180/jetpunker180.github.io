What are the five kingdoms of life? The chapter provides a detailed comparison of the Five-Kingdom System proposed by Robert Whittaker: Monera (prokaryotes/bacteria), Protista (single-celled eukaryotes), Fungi (decomposers like mushrooms and yeast), Plantae (multicellular autotrophs), and Animalia (multicellular heterotrophs). What are the three domains of life? You will also learn the modern Three-Domain System (Archaea, Bacteria, and Eukarya) and how it improves upon the five-kingdom model based on genetic evidence. What are the rules of binomial nomenclature?
