What is the gradient (slope) of a straight line? The gradient measures the steepness of a line and is defined as the ratio of vertical change to horizontal change between any two points on the line.

How do we calculate the slope between two points? Use the slope formula:

  • $m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}$
  • Example: For points $A(2,6)$ and $B(5,8)$, $m = \frac{8-6}{5-2} = \frac{2}{3}$
  • Example: For points $C(-2,4)$ and $D(1,-3)$, $m = \frac{-3-4}{1-(-2)} = \frac{-7}{3} = -\frac{7}{3}$

How do we relate slope to inclination angle? The slope $m$ equals the tangent of the inclination angle $\theta$ (the angle the line makes with the positive x-axis).

  • $m = \tan \theta$, where $0^\circ \leq \theta < 180^\circ$ and $\theta \neq 90^\circ$
  • For $\theta = 30^\circ$: $m = \tan 30^\circ = \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} \approx 0.577$
  • For $\theta = 60^\circ$: $m = \tan 60^\circ = \sqrt{3} \approx 1.732$
  • For $\theta = 45^\circ$: $m = \tan 45^\circ = 1$
  • For $\theta = 0^\circ$: $m = \tan 0^\circ = 0$ (horizontal line)
  • For $\theta = 90^\circ$: $m = \tan 90^\circ$ is undefined (vertical line)
  • For $\theta = 150^\circ$: $m = \tan 150^\circ = \tan(180^\circ - 30^\circ) = -\tan 30^\circ = -\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} \approx -0.577$

How do we find inclination angle from slope? Use inverse tangent and adjust to correct range:

  • $\theta = \tan^{-1}(m)$ gives an angle between $-90^\circ$ and $90^\circ$
  • If $m \geq 0$, $\theta = \tan^{-1}(m)$ (Quadrant I)
  • If $m < 0$, $\theta = 180^\circ + \tan^{-1}(m)$ (Quadrant II)
  • Example: $m = -0.577$, $\tan^{-1}(-0.577) = -30^\circ$, then $\theta = 180^\circ - 30^\circ = 150^\circ$

What is the condition for parallel lines? Two non-vertical lines are parallel if and only if their slopes are equal.

  • $m_1 = m_2$
  • Vertical lines are parallel to each other (both undefined slope)

What is the condition for perpendicular lines? Two non-vertical lines are perpendicular if and only if the product of their slopes is $-1$.

  • $m_1 \times m_2 = -1$
  • $m_2 = -\frac{1}{m_1}$ (negative reciprocal)
  • Example: If $m_1 = 2$, then perpendicular slope $m_2 = -\frac{1}{2}$
  • A horizontal line ($m = 0$) is perpendicular to a vertical line (undefined slope)

How do we prove collinearity using slopes? Three points $A$, $B$, and $C$ are collinear if the slope from $A$ to $B$ equals the slope from $B$ to $C$ (or $A$ to $C$).

  • $m_{AB} = m_{BC}$

How do we classify geometric shapes using slopes?

  • Right-angled triangle: Two sides are perpendicular ($m_1 \times m_2 = -1$)
  • Parallelogram: Both pairs of opposite sides have equal slopes (parallel)
  • Rectangle: Parallelogram with adjacent sides perpendicular
  • Square: Rectangle with all sides equal in length

How do we find missing vertices of a parallelogram? In a parallelogram, diagonals bisect each other. The intersection point is the midpoint of both diagonals.

  • If $A$, $B$, $C$ are known, find $D$ such that midpoint of $AC$ = midpoint of $BD$
  • $D = (x_A + x_C - x_B, y_A + y_C - y_B)$

What is the difference between medians and altitudes in triangles?

  • Median: Line from vertex to midpoint of opposite side. Find midpoint, then slope between vertex and midpoint
  • Altitude: Perpendicular line from vertex to opposite side. Slope is negative reciprocal of opposite side's slope

How do we solve for unknowns using parallel/perpendicular conditions? Set up equations using $m_1 = m_2$ for parallel or $m_1 \times m_2 = -1$ for perpendicular, then solve for the unknown variable (e.g., $k$, $x$).
