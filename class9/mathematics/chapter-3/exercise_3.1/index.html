What are Venn diagrams and set operations? Exercise 3.1, "Venn Diagrams and Set Operations," focuses on the visual representation and verification of fundamental set properties using overlapping circles.

How do we shade Venn diagrams for complex set operations? You will learn to shade Venn diagrams for operations such as:

  • $A \cup (B \cap C)$: Union of A with the intersection of B and C
  • $A \cap (B \cup C)$: Intersection of A with the union of B and C
  • $(A \cup B) \cap C$: Intersection of the union of A and B with C

Practice with different set configurations: overlapping sets, subsets, and disjoint sets.

What are the shading rules for Venn diagrams?

  • For union ($\cup$), shade all regions belonging to either set
  • For intersection ($\cap$), shade only regions common to both sets
  • When an intersection results in an empty set, indicate 'No Shading'

What are the Associative Laws of sets?

  • Associative Law of Union: $(A \cup B) \cup C = A \cup (B \cup C)$
  • Associative Law of Intersection: $(A \cap B) \cap C = A \cap (B \cap C)$

What are the Distributive Properties of sets?

  • Distributive Property of Intersection over Union: $X \cap (Y \cup Z) = (X \cap Y) \cup (X \cap Z)$
  • Distributive Property of Union over Intersection: $X \cup (Y \cap Z) = (X \cup Y) \cap (X \cup Z)$
