What are the different triangle construction methods? Triangles can be constructed using various criteria:

  • SAS (Side-Angle-Side): Two sides and the included angle are given
  • ASA (Angle-Side-Angle): Two angles and the included side are given
  • SSA (Side-Side-Angle): Two sides and a non-included angle are given (ambiguous case)
  • AAS (Angle-Angle-Side): Two angles and a non-included side are given
  • SSS (Side-Side-Side): All three sides are given

How do we construct a triangle using SAS method?

  • Step 1: Draw the base line segment of given length
  • Step 2: At one endpoint, construct the given angle using protractor or compass
  • Step 3: Measure the second side along the ray and mark the point
  • Step 4: Connect the endpoints to form the triangle

How do we construct a triangle using ASA method?

  • Step 1: Draw the base segment (the included side)
  • Step 2: At one endpoint, construct the first given angle
  • Step 3: At the other endpoint, construct the second given angle
  • Step 4: Extend the rays until they intersect — this intersection is the third vertex

How do we find a missing angle using angle sum property?

  • In any triangle, the sum of interior angles = $180^\circ$
  • If $\angle X$ and $\angle Z$ are given, then $\angle Y = 180^\circ - (\angle X + \angle Z)$
  • This is useful when an angle is missing for ASA or AAS constructions

How do we construct a triangle using SSA method (arc method)?

  • Step 1: Construct the given angle at one vertex
  • Step 2: Mark the adjacent side along one ray
  • Step 3: From the other endpoint of the base, draw an arc with radius equal to the opposite side length
  • Step 4: The intersection of the arc with the ray gives the third vertex
  • Note: SSA can have 0, 1, or 2 possible triangles (ambiguous case)

How do we construct specific angles using compass?

  • $60^\circ$: Construct an equilateral triangle (all sides equal)
  • $90^\circ$: Construct perpendicular bisector of a line segment
  • $45^\circ$: Bisect a $90^\circ$ angle
  • $75^\circ$: Construct $45^\circ + 30^\circ$
  • $120^\circ$: Construct $60^\circ + 60^\circ$ or supplementary to $60^\circ$

How do we construct a right-angled triangle?

  • Method 1: Construct a $90^\circ$ angle at one endpoint using perpendicular bisector, then measure the adjacent sides
  • Method 2: Use the property that the angle in a semicircle is $90^\circ$ (construct a circle with the hypotenuse as diameter)

What are the Steps of Construction? A formal geometric construction should include:

  • Step 1: Draw the base segment with given length
  • Step 2: Construct the required angle at the specified vertex
  • Step 3: Measure and mark the second side (if applicable)
  • Step 4: Draw arcs to locate the third vertex
  • Step 5: Join the vertices to complete the triangle
  • Step 6: Label all vertices ($A$, $B$, $C$) clearly

Important tips for constructions:

  • Construction arcs should be thin but clearly visible to show how vertices were located
  • Always verify that triangle labels match the problem description
  • Use a sharp pencil for accurate constructions
  • Do not erase construction arcs — they are part of the answer
