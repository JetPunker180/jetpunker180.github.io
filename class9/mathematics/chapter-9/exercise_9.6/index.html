How do we calculate perimeter for basic shapes? Use standard perimeter formulas:

  • Equilateral triangle: $P = 3s$ (where $s$ is side length)
  • Square: $P = 4s$
  • Regular polygon: $P = n \times s$ (where $n$ = number of sides)
  • Rectangle: $P = 2(l + w)$

How do we calculate area for various shapes?

  • Parallelogram: $A = b \times h$ (base × height)
  • Regular Pentagon: Divide into five congruent isosceles triangles from center, find area of one triangle × 5
  • Regular Hexagon: $A = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2} s^2$
  • Example: Hexagon with side $s = 10$ m: $A = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2} \times 100 = 150\sqrt{3} \approx 259.81$ m²

How do we calculate fencing cost? Fencing cost = Perimeter × rate per meter.

  • Example: Perimeter = $50$ m, rate = Rs. $220$ per meter → cost = $50 \times 220 = 11,000$ Rs

How do we calculate carpeting or flooring cost? Carpeting cost = Area × rate per square meter.

  • Example: Area = $25$ m², rate = Rs. $500$ per m² → cost = $25 \times 500 = 12,500$ Rs

How do we handle unit conversion (feet to meters)? $1$ foot = $0.3048$ meters.

  • To convert feet to meters: multiply by $0.3048$
  • To convert square feet to square meters: multiply by $(0.3048)^2 = 0.092903$
  • Example: Room side = $12$ ft = $12 \times 0.3048 = 3.6576$ m

How do we calculate total wall area for painting? For a square room:

  • Perimeter of room = $4 \times \text{side length}$
  • Total wall area = Perimeter × height = $4 \times \text{side} \times \text{height}$
  • Example: Room side = $10$ m, height = $3$ m → wall area = $4 \times 10 \times 3 = 120$ m²

How do we find perimeter of composite shapes (joined polygons)? When multiple polygons are joined together:

  • Sum the lengths of exterior sides only
  • Internal boundaries (shared sides) are NOT counted in the total perimeter
  • Example: Two hexagons joined along one side → count all sides except the shared side

How do we find the height of internal triangles in a regular pentagon? Use the Pythagorean theorem:

  • Divide pentagon into 5 congruent isosceles triangles
  • Find apothem (distance from center to midpoint of side) using trigonometry or Pythagorean theorem

How do we apply geometry to agricultural spaces?

  • Triangular lawn: Area = $\frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height}$, fencing = sum of three sides
  • Parallelogram cricket ground: Area = base × height, fencing = perimeter = $2(\text{base} + \text{side})$

Example - Composite shape perimeter: Two regular hexagons of side $5$ cm are joined along one full side. Find the perimeter of the combined shape.

  • Each hexagon has $6$ sides → total sides without joining = $12$ sides
  • When joined, $1$ side is shared (internal) → remove $2$ sides from total count
  • Exterior sides = $12 - 2 = 10$ sides
  • Total perimeter = $10 \times 5 = 50$ cm

Important note for costing problems: Always ensure units are consistent before calculating area or perimeter. Convert all measurements to the same unit (meters, feet, etc.) before applying formulas.
