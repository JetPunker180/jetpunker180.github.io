What are algebraic fractions? Exercise 4.6, "Algebraic Fractions," focuses on performing fundamental arithmetic operations on complex algebraic expressions (rational expressions).

What is the relationship between a fraction and its reciprocal? For any non-zero algebraic fraction:

  • Product of an algebraic fraction and its reciprocal = 1
  • If $\frac{P}{Q}$ is an algebraic fraction, its reciprocal is $\frac{Q}{P}$
  • $\frac{P}{Q} \times \frac{Q}{P} = 1$

How do we simplify algebraic fractions? Simplification requires factorizing numerators and denominators:

  • Step 1: Factorize numerator and denominator completely
  • Step 2: Cancel common factors
  • Step 3: Write the simplified fraction

What algebraic identities help factorize?

  • Difference of squares: $a^2 - b^2 = (a + b)(a - b)$
  • Square of sum: $(a + b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2$
  • Square of difference: $(a - b)^2 = a^2 - 2ab + b^2$
  • Difference of cubes: $a^3 - b^3 = (a - b)(a^2 + ab + b^2)$
  • Sum of cubes: $a^3 + b^3 = (a + b)(a^2 - ab + b^2)$

Example - Simplifying algebraic fractions: Simplify $\frac{x^2 - 9}{x^2 - 6x + 9}$

  • Numerator: $x^2 - 9 = (x - 3)(x + 3)$
  • Denominator: $x^2 - 6x + 9 = (x - 3)^2$
  • $\frac{x^2 - 9}{x^2 - 6x + 9} = \frac{(x - 3)(x + 3)}{(x - 3)^2} = \frac{x + 3}{x - 3}$

How do we multiply algebraic fractions? Multiply numerators together and denominators together, then simplify:

  • $\frac{P}{Q} \times \frac{R}{S} = \frac{P \times R}{Q \times S}$
  • Factorize before multiplying to simplify cancellation

How do we divide algebraic fractions? Division = multiply by the reciprocal of the divisor:

  • $\frac{P}{Q} \div \frac{R}{S} = \frac{P}{Q} \times \frac{S}{R} = \frac{P \times S}{Q \times R}$
  • Rule: Always flip the divisor (the second fraction)

Example - Division of algebraic fractions: Simplify $\frac{x^2 - 4}{x^2 - 1} \div \frac{x + 2}{x - 1}$

  • $\frac{x^2 - 4}{x^2 - 1} \times \frac{x - 1}{x + 2}$
  • $x^2 - 4 = (x - 2)(x + 2)$, $x^2 - 1 = (x - 1)(x + 1)$
  • $\frac{(x - 2)(x + 2)}{(x - 1)(x + 1)} \times \frac{x - 1}{x + 2} = \frac{x - 2}{x + 1}$

How do we add and subtract algebraic fractions? Find a common denominator:

  • $\frac{P}{Q} + \frac{R}{S} = \frac{P \times S + R \times Q}{Q \times S}$
  • $\frac{P}{Q} - \frac{R}{S} = \frac{P \times S - R \times Q}{Q \times S}$
  • Simplify numerator after combining

Example - Addition of algebraic fractions: Simplify $\frac{x}{x - 2} + \frac{3}{x + 2}$

  • Common denominator: $(x - 2)(x + 2)$
  • $\frac{x(x + 2) + 3(x - 2)}{(x - 2)(x + 2)} = \frac{x^2 + 2x + 3x - 6}{(x - 2)(x + 2)} = \frac{x^2 + 5x - 6}{(x - 2)(x + 2)}$
  • Check if numerator factorizes: $x^2 + 5x - 6 = (x + 6)(x - 1)$
  • No cancellation with denominator possible

What is middle-term breaking for algebraic fractions? When denominators are quadratic expressions like $x^2 - 4x - 21$, factorize using middle-term breaking:

  • $x^2 - 4x - 21 = (x - 7)(x + 3)$
  • Then use these factors to find common denominators

What does \"rearranging terms\" mean? Sometimes polynomials are not written in standard form. Rearrange in descending powers before factorizing:

  • Example: $6 - 7x - 5x^2$ becomes $-5x^2 - 7x + 6$
  • Then factorize as $-(5x^2 + 7x - 6) = -(5x - 3)(x + 2)$

How do we identify $a^3 - b^3$ identity in fractions? When you see expressions like $x^3 - 8$, recognize it as difference of cubes:

  • $x^3 - 8 = x^3 - 2^3 = (x - 2)(x^2 + 2x + 4)$
  • Similarly $x^3 + 27 = x^3 + 3^3 = (x + 3)(x^2 - 3x + 9)$

Key rules for algebraic fractions:

  • Always factorize completely before canceling
  • Division = multiply by reciprocal of divisor
  • For addition/subtraction, find LCM of denominators
  • Cancel only common factors (not common terms)
  • Final answer should be in simplest form

Common mistakes to avoid:

  • Forgetting to flip the divisor in division problems
  • Cancelling terms instead of factors (e.g., cancelling $x$ in $\frac{x+2}{x+3}$ is wrong)
  • Not factorizing denominators completely before finding common denominator
  • Sign errors when subtracting algebraic fractions (distribute negative sign!)
