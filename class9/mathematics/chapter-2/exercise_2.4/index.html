What is an antilogarithm? Exercise 2.4, "Antilogarithms," focuses on the reverse process of finding a number when its logarithm is already known. The antilogarithm is the inverse function of the common logarithm.

What is the relationship between log and antilog? If $\log x = y$, then the antilogarithm of $y$ is $x = 10^y$. In other words, $\text{antilog}(y) = 10^y$.

How do we find antilog using characteristic and mantissa? You will learn how to extract the characteristic and mantissa from a given logarithm to reconstruct the original number:

  • Step 1: Separate the logarithm into characteristic (integer part) and mantissa (decimal part)
  • Step 2: The characteristic determines the number of digits before the decimal point
  • Step 3: The mantissa determines the significant digits (found using antilog tables)

How does characteristic determine decimal placement?

  • Characteristic of 0 → number is between 1 and 10
  • Characteristic of 1 → number is between 10 and 100 (2 digits before decimal)
  • Characteristic of 2 → number is between 100 and 1000 (3 digits before decimal)
  • Characteristic of $n$ → number has $n+1$ digits before decimal
  • Characteristic of -1 (or $\bar{1}$) → number is between 0.1 and 1
  • Characteristic of -2 (or $\bar{2}$) → number is between 0.01 and 0.1

What is bar notation for negative characteristics? For antilog calculations, negative integers must be converted to bar notation:

  • -1 becomes $\bar{1}.0000$
  • -2 becomes $\bar{2}.0000$
  • -3 becomes $\bar{3}.0000$

Example - Antilog of a whole number: Find the antilog of 4.

  • $\text{antilog}(4) = 10^4 = 10,000$
  • Since characteristic = 4, the number has 5 digits: 10,000

Example - Antilog of a number with positive characteristic: If $\log x = 2.4718$, then:

  • Characteristic = 2 → number has 3 digits before decimal
  • Mantissa = 0.4718 → use antilog table to find significant digits
  • Result: $x \approx 297$ (rounded)

Example - Antilog of a number with bar notation: Find the antilog of $\bar{2}.0817$.

  • $\bar{2}$ means characteristic = -2
  • The number will be between 0.01 and 0.1
  • Using antilog table for mantissa 0.0817 gives approximately 1.207
  • With characteristic $\bar{2}$, the result is 0.01207

Example - Antilog of a negative integer: Find the antilog of -3.

  • First convert -3 to bar notation: $\bar{3}.0000$
  • $\text{antilog}(\bar{3}.0000) = 0.001$
  • Check: $\log(0.001) = \log(10^{-3}) = -3$ ✓

Key rules for antilogarithms:

  • Antilog is the inverse of logarithm: $\text{antilog}(\log x) = x$
  • For integers, antilog $n = 10^n$
  • Negative integers must be converted to bar notation before finding antilog
  • Bar notation $\bar{n}$ in antilog gives a result between $10^{-n}$ and $10^{-(n-1)}$
