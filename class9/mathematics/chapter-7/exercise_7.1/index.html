What is the distance formula? The distance formula is used to find the distance between two points in a Cartesian plane. It is derived from the Pythagorean theorem.

  • Formula: $d = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}$
  • Where $(x_1, y_1)$ and $(x_2, y_2)$ are the coordinates of two points
  • The result is always a non-negative real number

How do we calculate the distance between two points? Substitute the coordinates into the formula and simplify.

  • Example: Find the distance between $A(-2,1)$ and $B(1,5)$
  • $d = \sqrt{(1 - (-2))^2 + (5 - 1)^2} = \sqrt{(3)^2 + (4)^2} = \sqrt{9 + 16} = \sqrt{25} = 5$ units

How do we determine if three points are collinear? Three points are collinear if they lie on the same straight line. To verify:

  • Step 1: Calculate the distances $AB$, $BC$, and $AC$ using the distance formula
  • Step 2: Check if $AB + BC = AC$ (or any two smaller distances sum to the largest)
  • Example: For $A(0,1)$, $B(2,3)$, $C(3,4)$
  • $AB = \sqrt{(2-0)^2 + (3-1)^2} = \sqrt{4 + 4} = \sqrt{8} = 2\sqrt{2}$
  • $BC = \sqrt{(3-2)^2 + (4-3)^2} = \sqrt{1 + 1} = \sqrt{2}$
  • $AC = \sqrt{(3-0)^2 + (4-1)^2} = \sqrt{9 + 9} = \sqrt{18} = 3\sqrt{2}$
  • Since $AB + BC = 2\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{2} = 3\sqrt{2} = AC$, points are collinear ✓

How do we classify triangles using side lengths? After finding the three side lengths using the distance formula:

  • Equilateral triangle: All three sides equal
  • Isosceles triangle: Exactly two sides equal
  • Scalene triangle: All three sides different
  • Right triangle: Satisfies Pythagorean theorem $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$ (where $c$ is the largest side)

How do we find the perimeter of a triangle? Perimeter = sum of all three side lengths: $P = AB + BC + CA$

How do we prove that points form a square? For four points to form a square:

  • Condition 1: All four sides are equal in length
  • Condition 2: Both diagonals are equal in length
  • Note: All angles in a square are $90^\circ$, but we don't need to verify angles separately

How do we prove that points form a rectangle? For four points to form a rectangle:

  • Condition 1: Opposite sides are equal ($AB = CD$ and $BC = DA$)
  • Condition 2: Both diagonals are equal ($AC = BD$)

How do we prove that points form a parallelogram? For four points to form a parallelogram:

  • Opposite sides are equal ($AB = CD$ and $BC = DA$)
  • Note: Diagonals need not be equal (unlike rectangle)

Example - Proving a right triangle: Verify that $A(1,2)$, $B(4,6)$, $C(7,2)$ form a right triangle.

  • $AB = \sqrt{(4-1)^2 + (6-2)^2} = \sqrt{9 + 16} = \sqrt{25} = 5$
  • $BC = \sqrt{(7-4)^2 + (2-6)^2} = \sqrt{9 + 16} = \sqrt{25} = 5$
  • $AC = \sqrt{(7-1)^2 + (2-2)^2} = \sqrt{36 + 0} = \sqrt{36} = 6$
  • Check Pythagorean theorem: $5^2 + 5^2 = 25 + 25 = 50$, $6^2 = 36$ → $50 \neq 36$, so not right triangle (actually isosceles)

Radical simplification reminders:

  • $\sqrt{40} = \sqrt{4 \times 10} = 2\sqrt{10}$
  • $\sqrt{50} = \sqrt{25 \times 2} = 5\sqrt{2}$
  • $\sqrt{72} = \sqrt{36 \times 2} = 6\sqrt{2}$
