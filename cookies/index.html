Cookie Policy for NotesPrep

Last updated: April 6, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites place on your device (computer, smartphone, tablet) when you visit them. They help the website remember your actions and preferences over time.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to:

  • Understand how visitors use our website
  • Improve site performance and user experience
  • Analyze traffic and usage patterns
  • Monitor site security

3. What Cookies Do We Use?

8. Do Not Track (DNT) Signals

Some browsers have a "Do Not Track" feature. Our analytics services (Google Analytics, PostHog, Ahrefs) respect DNT signals. If DNT is enabled, we will not load tracking scripts.
