What is electric current? Electric current (I) is the rate of flow of electric charge. Mathematically, I = Q/t, where Q is charge in Coulombs and t is time in seconds. The SI unit of current is the Ampere (A), defined as one Coulomb of charge passing a point per second.

What is the difference between conventional and actual current? Conventional current is defined as the flow of positive charge from the positive to the negative terminal, whereas actual current is the flow of electrons from the negative to the positive terminal.

What is Ohm's Law? Ohm's Law states that the current through a conductor between two points is directly proportional to the voltage across the two points, provided the temperature remains constant. Mathematically, V = IR.

What is the formula for resistance? Resistance R = ρL/A, where ρ is resistivity (material property), L is length, and A is cross-sectional area. Resistance is directly proportional to length and resistivity, and inversely proportional to cross-sectional area.

What is the difference between Ohmic and Non-Ohmic devices?

| Type | I-V Relationship | Examples | |------|-----------------|----------| | Ohmic | Linear (straight line through origin) | Resistors, wires (at constant temperature) | | Non-Ohmic | Non-linear | Diodes, transistors, thermistors |

What is the difference between EMF and Potential Difference?

| Feature | EMF (Electromotive Force) | Potential Difference (PD) | |---------|---------------------------|--------------------------| | Definition | Total energy supplied per unit charge | Energy used between two points | | Circuit type | Open circuit | Closed circuit | | Value | Always greater than or equal to PD | Always ≤ EMF (due to internal resistance) |

How are ammeters and voltmeters connected in circuits?

  • Ammeter: Connected in series (low resistance) to measure current flow through the circuit.
  • Voltmeter: Connected in parallel (high resistance) to measure potential difference across a component.

Why is copper preferred for household wiring over silver? Although silver has slightly better conductivity, copper is used because it is much more cost-effective, has comparable durability, and is more resistant to corrosion at connection points.

What is an Ampere-hour (Ah)? Ampere-hour is a unit of electric charge. Q = I × t. For example, a 10 Ah battery can supply 10 A for 1 hour or 1 A for 10 hours.
