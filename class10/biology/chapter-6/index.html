What is the primary purpose of reproduction in living organisms? Reproduction is essential to ensure the continuation and survival of a species by passing genetic material to the next generation.

What is gametogenesis? Gametogenesis is the process of formation of gametes (sperm and egg cells).

  • Spermatogenesis: Occurs in the seminiferous tubules of the testes and produces four functional sperm cells per precursor cell. Begins at puberty and continues throughout life.
  • Oogenesis: Begins during fetal development and results in only one functional egg (ovum) and three polar bodies per precursor cell. The polar bodies degenerate.

How do hormones regulate the male reproductive system?

  • FSH (Follicle-Stimulating Hormone): Stimulates Sertoli cells to support sperm development and produce androgen-binding protein (ABP).
  • LH (Luteinizing Hormone): Stimulates Leydig cells to produce testosterone.
  • Testosterone: Maintains the high hormone levels necessary for spermatogenesis and development of male secondary sexual characteristics.

How is biological sex determined in humans? Sex is determined by the 23rd pair of chromosomes:

  • XX combination: Results in a female.
  • XY combination: Results in a male (the father''s Y-carrying sperm determines male offspring).
  • SRY gene (Sex-determining Region Y): Located on the Y chromosome, initiates the formation of testis-determining factor (TDF), driving male organ development.

What are the common methods of asexual reproduction in animals?

| Method | Description | Example | |--------|-------------|---------| | Binary fission | Parent splits into two equal halves | Amoeba, Paramecium | | Budding | New individual grows on parent and detaches | Hydra, Yeast | | Fragmentation | Body breaks into pieces, each becomes new individual | Planaria, Starfish | | Regeneration | Ability to regrow lost body parts | Starfish, Lizards (tail) | | Parthenogenesis | Offspring develop from unfertilized eggs | Bees, Ants, Aphids |

What are viviparous animals? Viviparous animals give birth to live young that have developed within the mother''s body and were nourished via a placenta. Examples include humans, dogs, cats, and cows.
