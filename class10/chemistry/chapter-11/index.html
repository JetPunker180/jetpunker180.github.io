What are carboxylic compounds? Carboxylic compounds are organic compounds containing the carboxyl functional group (-COOH). Carboxylic acids are weak acids that release H⁺ ions in solution and react with bases, metals, and carbonates.

What is ethanoic acid (acetic acid)? Ethanoic acid (CH₃COOH) is the simplest carboxylic acid. It is the primary component of vinegar (4-8% solution) and is widely used as a food preservative because its low pH inhibits microbial growth.

What is the product of the reaction between ethanoic acid and sodium carbonate? This reaction produces sodium acetate, water, and carbon dioxide gas (effervescence): 2CH₃COOH + Na₂CO₃ → 2CH₃COONa + CO₂↑ + H₂O

How do carboxylic acids react with active metals? They react with metals such as sodium (Na), potassium, and magnesium to form their respective salts and release hydrogen gas (H₂): 2CH₃COOH + 2Na → 2CH₃COONa + H₂↑

What is esterification? Esterification is a condensation reaction where a carboxylic acid reacts with an alcohol in the presence of concentrated sulfuric acid (catalyst and dehydrating agent) to form an ester and water. Example: CH₃COOH + C₂H₅OH ⇌ CH₃COOC₂H₅ (ethyl acetate) + H₂O

How is vinegar produced industrially? Vinegar is created through the bacterial oxidation of ethanol by Acetobacter bacteria in an aerobic environment, where enzymes catalyze the conversion to ethanoic acid. C₂H₅OH + O₂ → CH₃COOH + H₂O

How is ethanoic acid formed from ethanol via chemical oxidation? Ethanol is treated with an oxidizing agent like potassium manganate(VII) (KMnO₄), which provides oxygen to first remove hydrogen to form ethanal, then adds oxygen to form ethanoic acid. CH₃CH₂OH → CH₃CHO → CH₃COOH

What is the importance of esterification in the pharmaceutical industry? Esterification is used to create prodrugs like Aspirin (acetylsalicylic acid) and Valacyclovir that improve drug stability, increase bioavailability, and mask unpleasant tastes or odors.

How are esters utilized in the fragrance and cosmetic industries? Due to their pleasant, fruity aromas (e.g., isoamyl acetate smelling like banana), esters are used in perfumes. As emollients like isopropyl myristate, they soften skin and improve the texture of lotions.

Summary of ethanoic acid reactions:

| Reaction with | Product | Observation | |---------------|---------|-------------| | Sodium carbonate | Sodium acetate + CO₂ + H₂O | Effervescence (bubbles) | | Sodium metal | Sodium acetate + H₂ | Bubbles of hydrogen gas | | Alcohol (esterification) | Ester + Water | Sweet/fruity smell | | Base (NaOH) | Sodium acetate + Water | Neutralization (heat released) |
