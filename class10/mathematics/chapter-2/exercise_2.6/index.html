How are real-world verbal relationships translated into solvable quadratic models? Mathematical word problems require translating descriptive constraints systematically into single-variable algebraic equations.

Consecutive Even Numbers: Consecutive even integers always differ by exactly two units. If the first even value is $x$, the next consecutive even value is $x+2$. Product equation: $x(x+2) = 120$ → $x^2 + 2x - 120 = 0$ → $(x-10)(x+12)=0$ → $x=10$ or $x=-12$. Solution sets: $\{10, 12\}$ or $\{-12, -10\}$.

Number and Square Differences: If the difference between a positive number's square and the number itself equals 380: $x^2 - x = 380$ → $x^2 - x - 380 = 0$ → $(x-20)(x+19)=0$ → $x=20$ or $x=-19$. Since the problem specifies a positive number, reject $x=-19$ → $x=20$.

How do we solve for uniform surrounding borders? Postage stamp: $4$ cm × $5$ cm (area $= 20$ cm²). Total paper area $= 5\frac{1}{2} \times 20 = 110$ cm². Uniform border width $x$ added to all sides: new dimensions $(4+2x)$ and $(5+2x)$.

Equation: $(4+2x)(5+2x) = 110$

  • $20 + 8x + 10x + 4x^2 = 110$
  • $4x^2 + 18x + 20 = 110$
  • $4x^2 + 18x - 90 = 0$
  • Divide by 2: $2x^2 + 9x - 45 = 0$
  • Quadratic formula: $x = \frac{-9 \pm \sqrt{81 + 360}}{4} = \frac{-9 \pm \sqrt{441}}{4} = \frac{-9 \pm 21}{4}$
  • $x = \frac{12}{4} = 3$ or $x = \frac{-30}{4} = -7.5$ (reject negative)
  • Border width $x = 3$ cm
