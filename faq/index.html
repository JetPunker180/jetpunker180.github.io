Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Last updated: April 6, 2026

General Questions

1. What is NotesPrep?

NotesPrep is a free educational platform for secondary school students and entry test aspirants. We provide study notes, entry test guides (NUST, ECAT, NET), blog posts, and mock tests—all completely free.

2. Who is NotesPrep for?

Our resources are designed for secondary school students (grades 9–10) and entry test aspirants. We do not currently offer resources for higher secondary (HSSC) students.

3. Do I need to sign up?

No. NotesPrep does not require any sign up, account, or registration. All resources are freely accessible to everyone.

Downloading Notes

5. How do I download notes?

Navigate to the main page, find your topic, click "Download Notes," view a short ad, and your download will start automatically.

7. Why do I see ads before downloading?

Short ads help keep NotesPrep completely free for all students. They only appear during downloads, not during browsing or mock tests.

Mock Tests & Entry Tests

11. Are answer keys provided?

Yes. Our entry test MCQ bank includes detailed answer explanations for every question.
