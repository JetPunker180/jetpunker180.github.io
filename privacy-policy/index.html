Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 13, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to our students and learners. This includes:

  • Personal Information: When you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address.
  • Usage Data: Information on how you interact with our notes, such as page views and time spent on specific chapters.
  • Device Information: Technical data such as your IP address, browser type, and operating system.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data helps us maintain the quality of NotesPrep. We use it to:

  • Deliver the study materials and notes you request.
  • Send periodic updates regarding new 2026 entry test materials.
  • Analyze site traffic to see which subjects (like Math or Physics) need more resources.
  • Prevent fraudulent activity and ensure site security.

3. Third-Party Services

We share non-identifiable data with trusted third parties to help us run our platform efficiently:

  • Google Analytics: To understand visitor demographics and behavior.
  • PostHog: To record sessions and improve our UI/UX based on student movement.
  • Ahrefs: To monitor our SEO performance and search visibility.

4. Data Retention

We retain your personal information (like email addresses for newsletters) only for as long as necessary to provide you with our services. Usage data is typically aggregated and kept for analytical purposes to improve our educational content.

5. Your Rights

As a user of NotesPrep, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request that we correct or delete your data.
  • Unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time using the "Unsubscribe" link in our emails.
